# Support

Email **support@keyflint.dev** from the address you bought with, or reply to your license email.
Include your license key prefix (for example `LIC-INDIE-eyJ...`, never the full key in public
issues), what you ran, and the error code you got.

| Plan | First response |
| :--- | :--- |
| Indie, Founder, Pro | Within 2 business days |
| Agency | Within 1 business day (priority queue) |
| Community | GitHub issues, best effort |

Business days: Monday to Friday, CET.

## Self-service first

- **Lost license email:** check spam, then write to us; we resend it to your purchase address.
- **Refund:** within 14 days of purchase, no questions asked. The license is revoked when the
  refund is processed.
- **Bugs and feature requests:** [GitHub issues](https://github.com/ArceeDev/keyflint/issues).
- **Security issues:** email support@keyflint.dev with "SECURITY" in the subject. Do not open a
  public issue.
