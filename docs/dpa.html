Data Processing Addendum (DPA)

KeyFlint Managed · Version 1.2 · Pursuant to Article 28 GDPR
This Data Processing Addendum ("DPA") governs the processing of personal data on behalf of customers who subscribe to KeyFlint Managed (Cloud editions) to issue cryptographic licenses and meter usage for their end users.

Note for Self-Hosted Customers: If you run the open-source or self-hosted edition of KeyFlint on your own infrastructure (Founder, Indie, or Agency licenses), KeyFlint processes zero end-user data and this DPA does not apply to your operations.

1. Parties and Scope

Data Controller ("Customer"): The legal entity or individual subscribed to KeyFlint Managed.
Data Processor ("KeyFlint"): KeyFlint (ArceeDev). Contact: support@keyflint.dev.

KeyFlint provides a managed licensing gateway, Ed25519 digital signature service, and atomic quota-metering backend. In delivering this service, KeyFlint processes personal data strictly on behalf of and under documented instructions from the Customer pursuant to Article 28 of Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR).

2. Details of Processing (GDPR Article 28.3)

Parameter Specification
Subject Matter & Nature Hosting, encrypted storage, Ed25519 digital signing, cryptographic verification, quota metering, and automated deletion.
Purpose Issuance and renewal of license tokens, off-chain and online verification, atomic credit debits and refunds, and disaster recovery backup replication.
Categories of Data Subjects End users and buyers of the Customer's software applications, CLI utilities, and Model Context Protocol (MCP) servers.
Categories of Personal Data Buyer email addresses, license IDs, node-locking cryptographic hardware hashes (if enabled), quota balances, and API request audit metadata. Special categories of data (GDPR Article 9) and payment card numbers are strictly prohibited and never processed.

3. Processor Obligations

  • Documented Instructions: Process personal data only on documented instructions from the Customer, including regarding transfers of personal data outside the EEA, unless required to do so by Union or Member State law.
  • Confidentiality: Ensure that all personnel and automated processes authorized to administer the infrastructure are bound by strict obligations of confidentiality.
  • Security of Processing (Art. 32): Implement state-of-the-art technical and organizational measures, including TLS 1.3 transport encryption, isolated per-tenant containers (Fly.io), encrypted continuous WAL backups (Litestream on Cloudflare R2), and cryptographic Ed25519 validation.
  • Assistance to Controller: Provide reasonable assistance to the Customer in responding to requests by data subjects exercising their rights under Chapter III of the GDPR.
  • Security Breach Notification: Notify the Customer without undue delay, and in any event within 48 hours, upon confirming any personal data breach affecting Customer data.

4. Authorized Sub-processors

The Customer grants general authorization for the following essential infrastructure sub-processors:

Sub-processor Role & Function Data Location Transfer Safeguards
Fly.io, Inc. Application container runtime and NVMe volume execution European Union (Paris / Frankfurt) Data Privacy Framework (DPF) / Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs)
Cloudflare, Inc. Continuous Litestream backup object storage (Cloudflare R2) European Union jurisdiction Data Privacy Framework (DPF) / Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs)
Resend Transactional email delivery for customer license delivery Global TLS transit Data Privacy Framework (DPF) / Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs)

5. Data Deletion and Service Termination

Upon cancellation or expiration of KeyFlint Managed services, the Customer is granted a 7-day grace period to export database snapshots and active public keys. Following this grace period, KeyFlint permanently deletes the customer's Fly.io machine, persistent volume, and Litestream backup replicas within 30 days in accordance with secure automated decommissioning protocols.

6. Governing Law

This DPA shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of Spain and the European Union.
