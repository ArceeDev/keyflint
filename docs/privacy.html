Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026
Draft pending legal review. This page is an English draft and has not yet been reviewed by a lawyer. It does not constitute legal advice.
This policy describes the personal data KeyFlint processes as a controller (website, license purchases and delivery, and technical support). Processing of end-user data that results from using the hosted KeyFlint Managed service is governed by the Data Processing Agreement (DPA).

1. Controller

Service owner: KeyFlint (ArceeDev)
Privacy and support contact: support@keyflint.dev
Main jurisdiction: Spain / European Union (GDPR, Regulation (EU) 2016/679, and the Spanish LOPDGDD).

2. Data, Purposes and Legal Bases

Context Data Purpose Legal basis Retention
Website visits (keyflint.dev) IP address and technical connection metadata Server security, content delivery and aggregate, cookieless traffic statistics Legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) Technical access logs (up to 90 days)
License purchase (self-hosted or Managed) Email, name, country, payment-provider customer ID Issuing, cryptographically delivering and supporting the license Performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) License term + statutory tax retention periods
Managed instance Contact email, assigned domain/slug, public keys Provisioning and operating the infrastructure Performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) Service term + 7-day grace period after cancellation
Transactional emails Email address and the content of the license or quota alerts Product delivery and operational notifications Performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) Delivery logs at the email provider (up to 30 days)
Technical support Email address and messages you send Resolving incidents and providing technical assistance Legitimate interest and the contractual relationship 2 years from the last message

Payment data: KeyFlint never accesses or stores card numbers. Payments are processed by Stripe (Stripe Managed Payments acts as Merchant of Record), which is PCI-DSS Level 1 certified.

Cookies: This static website sets no advertising or tracking cookies and uses no third-party fingerprinting. Traffic statistics come from Cloudflare Web Analytics, which does not use cookies.

3. Recipients and Providers (Sub-processors)

We rely on the following technology providers, each bound by security and confidentiality commitments:

  • Stripe, Inc. / Stripe Payments Europe: payments, invoicing and Merchant of Record.
  • Fly.io, Inc.: compute and container hosting (servers in the EU region: Paris/Frankfurt).
  • Cloudflare, Inc.: DNS, email forwarding, cookieless web analytics, and continuous backups (Litestream) on Cloudflare R2 in the EU jurisdiction.
  • Resend: transactional license emails sent over TLS.
  • GitHub, Inc.: hosting of the source repository and public documentation.

4. International Transfers

KeyFlint Managed database servers and backups are located in the European Union. Where auxiliary providers operate from outside the European Economic Area, transfers rely on the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework (DPF) or on the Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) adopted by the European Commission.

5. Your Rights

Under the GDPR and applicable law, you have the right to access your personal data, request its rectification or erasure, restrict or object to its processing, and request its portability.

To exercise these rights, email support@keyflint.dev. We will respond within 30 days at the latest. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency (www.aepd.es) or the supervisory authority in your country.

6. End Users of Tools Built with KeyFlint

If you are an end user of an MCP server, CLI or application that a developer protects with KeyFlint, the controller is the developer or company you bought the product from. KeyFlint does not store your usage data except on that developer's behalf under its contract with them.
