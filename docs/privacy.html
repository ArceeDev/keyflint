Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026
This Privacy Policy describes the personal data that KeyFlint processes as a Data Controller (website visitors, customer accounts, license delivery, and technical support). The processing of end-user data on behalf of customers using KeyFlint Managed is governed by our Data Processing Addendum (DPA).

1. Data Controller

Service Provider: KeyFlint (ArceeDev)
Privacy & Support Contact: support@keyflint.dev
Primary Jurisdiction: Spain / European Union (in accordance with Regulation (EU) 2016/679 - GDPR).

2. Data Processing Activities, Purposes and Legal Bases

Context Categories of Data Purpose Legal Basis (GDPR) Retention Period

Payment Security: KeyFlint never collects, processes, or stores credit or debit card numbers. All payment checkouts are securely executed by PCI-DSS Level 1 certified billing processors (Stripe / Lemon Squeezy).

Cookies & Tracking: Our public website does not use tracking cookies, behavioral ad pixels, or third-party device fingerprinting scripts. Web analytics are privacy-first, aggregated, and cookieless via Cloudflare Web Analytics.

3. Third-Party Service Providers (Sub-processors)

We work with trusted infrastructure providers that operate under strict data protection and confidentiality agreements:

  • Stripe, Inc. / Lemon Squeezy: Payment processing, checkout infrastructure, and Merchant of Record compliance.
  • Fly.io, Inc.: Application compute and container runtime (European Union regions: Paris / Frankfurt).
  • Cloudflare, Inc. (Cloudflare R2): Encrypted continuous backup storage (Litestream) located within EU jurisdiction.
  • Resend: Secure transactional email delivery for cryptographic license fulfillment over TLS.
  • GitHub, Inc. (Microsoft): Open-source repository hosting and static documentation delivery.

4. International Data Transfers

Primary servers, SQLite databases, and Litestream replicas for KeyFlint are hosted within data centers inside the European Union. In cases where auxiliary service providers process metadata outside the European Economic Area (EEA), transfers are protected under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework (DPF) or the European Commission's approved Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs).

5. Your Rights as a Data Subject

Under Chapter III of the GDPR, you have the right to request access to your personal data, rectification of inaccurate records, erasure ("right to be forgotten"), restriction of processing, objection to processing, and data portability.

To exercise any of these rights, contact our privacy contact directly at support@keyflint.dev. We respond to all verified requests within 30 days. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency (www.aepd.es) or your local supervisory authority.

6. End Users of Tools Protected by KeyFlint

If you are an end user of an MCP server, CLI, or software tool sold by a developer using KeyFlint, that developer is the independent Data Controller of your account and licensing relationship. KeyFlint processes usage and quota deduction events exclusively on the developer's behalf under our Data Processing Addendum.
