How it works

From purchase to a paid call in 4 steps

  1. 1

    Deploy the gateway

    One Docker container with a SQLite volume. No Postgres, no Redis.

  2. 2

    Connect your payments

    Every Stripe or LemonSqueezy purchase issues the license via signed webhook and emails it.

  3. 3

    Verify offline

    Your app checks the Ed25519 token locally with keyflint-verify in under 1 ms.

  4. 4

    Charge per use

    Each call debits credits with @metered. If the tool fails, they are refunded automatically.
